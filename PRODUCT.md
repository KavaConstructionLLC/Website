# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Homeowners in and around Morganton, NC (roughly 40 miles out) with a roof, kitchen, bath, deck, porch, flooring or concrete job. Most arrive on a phone, often mid-problem (a leak, a dated kitchen), comparing two or three local contractors. English and Spanish speakers. They want to know: is this crew real, local, licensed, and will they show up and give me a straight price.

## Product Purpose
Kava Construction LLC (formerly Kava Home Improvements) is a licensed, insured, 10+ year home contractor. The site exists to turn a visit into a free-estimate request or booked call. Single conversion goal: estimate request / booking through the existing wizard and Calendly, with tap-to-call and text as the always-available backup. Success is more booked estimates, not time on page.

## Brand Personality
Local crew, plainspoken, earned confidence. Three words: grounded, sharp, trustworthy. Feels like a good tradesperson's truck and tape measure, not an agency. Proof is real job photos and real details, not slogans.

## Anti-references
- Generic template contractor sites: stock-photo heroes, icon-card grids, "Quality You Can Trust" copy.
- Flashy or gimmicky: heavy 3D, parallax everywhere, slow intros.
- Cold corporate or luxury: it must still feel like a local crew a Morganton homeowner can call.

## Design Principles
1. Every screen has one next step: get the free estimate. Call and text are always one tap away on mobile.
2. Real work is the proof. Lead with the crew's own photos; never fake or generic imagery.
3. Craft shows in the details (type, spacing, joinery between sections), the way good carpentry does, without decoration for its own sake.
4. Motion earns its place and never costs speed: a short, confident intro, then restraint. Fast on a mid-range phone on LTE.
5. Local specificity beats claims: name the towns served, the street, the phone number, the language spoken.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast minimum, large tap targets (56px CTAs), visible focus, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (intro is skipped or becomes an instant state). Bilingual cues (Se habla español) kept visible. No information carried by color alone.
